Help and FAQ

Quick answers for common questions.

Can I use Kahera without an internet connection?

Yes. Kahera is designed for offline use during busy sales periods. Sync your latest data when your connection returns.

What does Kahera Pro include?

Pro includes unlimited inventory, unlimited orders, cloud sync, advanced reporting, and watermark-free PDF exports.

How do I get help with a subscription?

Contact support with the email used for your account and a short description of the billing or upgrade issue. If you subscribed through an app store, subscription changes may also need to be managed through that store account.

How does Kahera handle privacy and business data?

Kahera uses cloud backup and sync features to keep your business data available across devices. Contact support for privacy requests, data access questions, or account deletion help.

Can I export reports?

Yes. Kahera supports daily profit reports and PDF exports. Pro removes PDF watermarks and unlocks advanced reporting.
